With Angel gone and Hank dividing his time between the team and genetic research, the X-Men struggle to integrate Havok and Polaris. Mutant abductions lead them to the Morlocks and to Hank’s mentor, whose experiments connect the team’s fractured relationships to a much larger exploitation of mutant life.
