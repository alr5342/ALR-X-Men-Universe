During a televised debate, Xavier wins over the audience. Stryker suspects telepathic interference and activates the inhibitor, overloading Xavier’s mind.

Jean cannot reach him. Wolverine argues for war, Storm urges caution and Cyclops must lead through the crisis. Xavier is later tethered to Cerebro as part of the extermination scheme.
