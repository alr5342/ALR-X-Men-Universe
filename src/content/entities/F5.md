Apocalypse, backed by Sinister, turns Angel, Beast, Iceman and Jean Grey into his Four Horsemen. He claims the right to rule mutantkind through survival of the fittest, treating weaker mutants as expendable. The expanded X-Men community, including the integrated Acolytes and New Mutants, faces him with humanity generally trusting the team to defend it. Apocalypse is definitively defeated, but his plan partly succeeds through a mass awakening or emergence of mutants around the world.
