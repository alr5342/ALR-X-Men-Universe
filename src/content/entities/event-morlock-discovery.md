The X-Men intervene when Harpoon and Random attack Madin. Jean tracks the escaping mutant underground, where Callisto and Caliban explain the disappearances.

The encounter introduces a community whose exclusion differs from life at the mansion. Jean offers refuge; the subsequent Marauder attack turns first contact into a shared crisis.
