Mojo abducts Cyclops, Jean, Beast, Iceman, Havok and Polaris to keep their lives profitable and their conflicts unresolved. Xavier recruits a global rescue team, but its intervention gives Mojo an opportunity to replace the original stars. The captives’ wish to choose their own futures becomes inseparable from their rescuers’ refusal to be used as replacements.
