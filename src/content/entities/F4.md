Kelly’s Sentinel program and Stryker’s religious influence turn public hostility into a national mutant crisis. When an attack puts Xavier in a coma, Cyclops must lead an expanded X-Men team without him. Discovering a scheme to use Cerebro for extermination forces an alliance with Magneto’s Acolytes, on terms that preserve the team’s refusal to become executioners.
