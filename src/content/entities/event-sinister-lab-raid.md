Callisto and Leech join the X-Men’s return to the laboratory. Leech dampens the Marauders’ powers, Polaris destroys the research and the Summers brothers combine their attacks against Blockbuster.

Sinister escapes. Some Morlocks subsequently move into the mansion while others remain below; his later recovery of part of the formula preserves the threat beyond this defeat.
