---

Xavier’s school offers young mutants education and control while hiding its real work from a fearful public. Bobby Drake joins the original class as Domino’s Brotherhood attacks MRD transports to liberate detained mutants. The X-Men must decide how to protect people without endorsing either the hunters’ treatment of mutants or the destruction left by the liberators.
