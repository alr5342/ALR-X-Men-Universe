Storm agrees to help without becoming Xavier’s soldier. The recruits come from Kenya, Canada, Russia and Germany, with Forge providing the technological route into Mojoworld.

Their mission expands when Storm insists on rescuing the production system’s other captives. Longshot becomes their ally from within.
