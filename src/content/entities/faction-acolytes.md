Magneto's Film 4 organization includes Rogue, Gambit and potentially additional members. It is completely unrelated to Domino's Film 1 Brotherhood.

The group folds into the main X-Men by Film 4's end. Acolytes is a provisional, changeable name; its identity and integration are established.
